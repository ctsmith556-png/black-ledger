# Vertical Slice — The Surgeon: Full Script

Voice direction: soft, clinical, precise. Medical vocabulary even in casual moments. Rarely shouts — and when he does, it is terrifying. Reference: Sam Neill in *Event Horizon*, quieter.

## 2. In-Match Barks

Keep each line short and dry. Rotate randomly within a category; never repeat back-to-back.

**Character select**
- "Dr. Eli Vance. I used to save people."
- "I have rehearsed this. To the minute."

**Match start**
- "Triage. Let's see who's bleeding."
- "I'll be brief. So will you."

**Signature Special ready (Operating Table)**
- "The table is prepped."
- "Hold still. This will help."

**Special used — diagnosing an enemy**
- "There. I can see exactly where you'll fail."
- "Exposed. Textbook."

**Killing an opponent**
- "Time of death, noted."
- "I did everything I could. (I didn't.)"
- "Closed."

**Took heavy damage**
- "Hemorrhaging. Compensating."
- "That will scar."

**Near death**
- "Vitals... critical. Not yet. Not yet."
- "I am not done. I am never done."

**Health pickup**
- "Sutured."
- "Stable. For now."

**Victory**
- "The patient lived. This time."

**Defeat**
- "...flatline." *(the cabin's heart monitor holds a single tone)*

**Idle / waiting**
- "Every second you wait, something in you is dying. Medically speaking."

**Vs. The Foundryman (Mill boss)**
- "Burns. Third-degree, full thickness. Survivable — if I'm fast."

## 3. The Mill — Chapter Lines (the Auditor)

Soft, polite, unhurried. He narrates from nowhere.

- On arena load: "The Mill. Eleven men died here, once. He blames himself. Doctor — show me how you handle the ones you cannot save."
- Boss intro (THE FOUNDRYMAN rises from Furnace 3 through the slag): **THE FOUNDRYMAN:** "You're early for the pour."
- On boss defeat: **THE AUDITOR:** "His debt is settled. His vehicle is yours. The account, as always, balances."

# Vertical Slice — Opponent Barks

Bark sets for the five characters who serve as the slice's AI opponents (and the next roster voices to record), plus expanded boss taunts for The Foundryman. The Surgeon (player) is covered in `THE_SURGEON_SCRIPT.md`. Keep every line short; rotate within a category; never repeat back-to-back. Voice refs are from Design Bible §3.

---

## Hollow — amnesiac arsonist
*Voice: quiet, halting, like a damaged throat. Avoids pronouns ("the fire," "the small ones"). Long pauses. Ref: Doug Bradley, dialed down.*

- **Select:** "The fire is hungry. ...It remembers you."
- **Match start:** "Everything... burns. Eventually."
- **Special ready (Conflagration):** "The fire wants out."
- **Special used:** "Feed it. ...Feed it everything."
- **Kill:** "Ash. ...Only ash now." / "Not the small ones. ...You."
- **Took heavy damage:** "...It hurts. The fire always hurts."
- **Near death:** "Don't... let it go out."
- **Health pickup:** "Not done burning."
- **Victory:** "The house is warm now."
- **Defeat:** "...cold. So cold." *(the fire dying)*
- **Idle:** "Do you smell smoke? ...You will."

## Miss Antoinette — faded beauty
*Voice: honeyed Southern drawl, performatively warm with claws underneath; obsessed with looks. Ref: Jessica Lange.*

- **Select:** "Well, aren't you a sight. Pity."
- **Match start:** "Smile for me, darlin'. It's the last pretty thing you'll do."
- **Special ready (Powder Compact):** "Let me just... freshen up."
- **Special used (reflect):** "Sugar, that's not becoming. Have it back."
- **Kill:** "Bless your heart, you photographed so poorly." / "A face the world won't miss."
- **Took heavy damage:** "You'll pay for that scratch."
- **Near death:** "Not the face. NOT the face—"
- **Health pickup:** "Good as new. Better."
- **Victory:** "Beauty wins. It always wins."
- **Defeat:** "Don't look at me. Don't you DARE look at me."
- **Idle:** "Most beautiful woman in three states, once."

## The Warden — authority, one mistake too many
*Voice: gravel, terse, military cadence; prison jargon. Ref: J.K. Simmons, older and quieter.*

- **Select:** "Reilly. Thirty-one years. I know how this ends."
- **Match start:** "Lights out. Move."
- **Special ready (Solitary):** "Chain's hot."
- **Special used:** "You're in the hole. Sit tight." / "Restrained. Don't fight it."
- **Kill:** "Sentence served." / "Should've listened."
- **Took heavy damage:** "That all?"
- **Near death:** "Not in the yard. Not like Voss."
- **Health pickup:** "Back on the count."
- **Victory:** "Cell's closed. We're done."
- **Defeat:** "Transfer me. Get me out—"
- **Idle:** "Every man here says he's innocent. Even me."

## Pup — innocent-seeming horror
*Voice: a child's, recorded at slightly the wrong speed; third person. Ref: child VO + pitch/reverse processing.*

- **Select:** "Pup wants to play. Do you want to play?"
- **Match start:** "Ready or not..."
- **Special ready (Storytime):** "Pup knows a story. It's a scary one."
- **Special used (invert):** "Which way is up? Pup forgot. Did you?"
- **Kill:** "You're it. No backsies." / "Pup wins. Pup always wins."
- **Took heavy damage:** "Ow. That's not nice."
- **Near death:** "Pup wants to go home. Pup wants to go HOME."
- **Health pickup:** "All better!"
- **Victory:** "Again! Let's go again!"
- **Defeat:** "Don't leave Pup alone. Please don't—"
- **Idle:** "The truck is cold inside. Want to see?"

## Crucible — working-class survivor guilt
*Voice: heavy Rust Belt accent; reserved, fierce when provoked; "my men," "the boys." Ref: Brian Cox-type gravel.*

- **Select:** "Bessemer. Twenty-eight years on the floor. Get in."
- **Match start:** "Pour's startin'. Stay outta the way."
- **Special ready (Pour):** "She's ready to tap."
- **Special used:** "Watch the slag. Burns right through ya."
- **Kill:** "Shoulda evacuated." / "That's on me too."
- **Took heavy damage:** "Mill steel. Takes more'n that."
- **Near death:** "Not goin' down. Not 'til I make it right."
- **Health pickup:** "Patched. Back to work."
- **Victory:** "For the boys. All eleven."
- **Defeat:** "Finish the pour. I said FINISH the—"
- **Idle:** "I gave the order. Every night I give it again."

## BOSS — The Foundryman "Tap" (expanded)
*Voice: a furnace roar with words in it; speaks in temperatures and pours. Ref: §6.1.*

- **Encounter:** "You're early for the pour."
- **Phase 2 (66%):** "Hotter now. Can you feel it?"
- **Phase 3 / overheat (33%):** "I am the furnace. I am the FURNACE."
- **Lands a heavy hit:** "Down you go. Into the heat."
- **Player near death:** "Slag and bone. No difference to me."
- **On defeat:** "...cooling. Finally... cooling."

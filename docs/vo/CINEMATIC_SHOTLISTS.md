# Cinematic Shot-Lists — Key Set

Pivotal cinematics formalized into shot-lists (the Surgeon's are in `THE_SURGEON_SCRIPT.md`). The other characters' intros/endings live as prose in Design Bible §3 and can be formalized the same way on demand. Play every ending straight — let the silence carry the cost (Bible §2.3).

## Hollow — Ending (~60–90s)
| # | Shot | Audio / VO |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | The Auditor walks him down a suburban street he doesn't know — but his hands shake. They stop at a two-story house. | Quiet. |
| 2 | The Auditor opens the door. Hollow steps in. He remembers. | **AUDITOR:** "The entry has been struck." |
| 3 | Flash: he set the fire for the insurance, to stop the foreclosure. He didn't know they hadn't gotten out. Two small voices were his daughters. | Fire, muffled; two faint cries. |
| 4 | He looks down. His hands are unburned. He is a young man again. | — |
| 5 | From upstairs, very faintly: two little girls laughing. He starts toward the stairs. | Laughter. |
| 6 | The Auditor closes the door behind him. We never see what is upstairs. Hold black. | Silence... then, softly, the sound of fire. |

## Miss Antoinette — Ending (~60–90s)
| # | Shot | Audio / VO |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | The Auditor presents a single ornate silver hand mirror. | **AUDITOR:** "The entry has been struck." |
| 2 | She looks in: twenty-three again, radiant, her magazine-cover face. She weeps with joy. | A held breath. |
| 3 | She races home, tears the cloth off every mirror — twenty-three in all of them. | Glass; her laughter. |
| 4 | That night she catches her reflection in a dark window pane: still seventy-one. The mirrors lie. | A single discordant note. |
| 5 | Everyone who looks at her sees youth. She alone sees the truth. She covers every reflective surface. | — |
| 6 | Six months later she shatters every mirror she owns. Final shot: she sits in the dark among the shards — and the broken glass shows a young woman, smiling back. | Breaking glass, then quiet. |

## Hemlock — Ending (~60–90s)
| # | Shot | Audio / VO |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | The Auditor meets her on the bank of the Ash River — clean now, silver in the sun. | **AUDITOR:** "The entry has been struck." |
| 2 | She's back in the third year, across a desk from a federal attorney who finally believes her. No sabotage. No breach. | Office hum. |
| 3 | Montage: the case drags nine years — injunctions, appeals, "discharge pending review." She watches the cancer cluster widen from the gallery. | Gavels; a ventilator; rain. |
| 4 | The boy, Theo, lives — grows up two streets from the plant, drinks the water, is diagnosed at nineteen. | A child laughing, then a heart monitor. |
| 5 | Her one act of violence had been the only thing that ever made them stop in time. | **AUDITOR:** "You wished to take the water back. It had to go somewhere." |
| 6 | Final shot: Hemlock, older, alone at the culvert — running clear now. Nothing left to carry. The river is clean and the children are still sick and no one will ever know why. | Clean water. Silence. |

## Vault — Intro (montage) & Ending (the true ending)
**Intro montage:** cryptic images — a different tournament, a different decade; a different dying city; a man in a charcoal suit; a figure in black walking away from a burning building and climbing into an armored car. **TITLE CARD: VAULT.**

| # | Ending shot | Audio / VO |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | The Auditor sits across from him. | **AUDITOR:** "You knew the cost. You knew you could not be paid." |
| 2 | Vault speaks — the first time. | **VAULT:** "I came to balance the Ledger." |
| 3 | He places his hand on the book. Every contestant's page tears itself free — blank now. | Pages tearing; a rising hum. |
| 4 | The Auditor's form flickers. He was never a person — only the Ledger's enforcement, and the Ledger is closed. | **AUDITOR:** "Then I am free as well." |
| 5 | He dissipates. The streetlights stop flickering; the radios resume normal broadcast. | The city exhales. |
| 6 | Vault drives out of Ashen Hollow down a road lit by dawn. He does not look back. The empty city, at peace. Cut to credits. | Engine fading into morning. |

## The Auditor — Final Boss / Finale (~the climax)
| # | Shot | Audio / VO |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | No arena loads. The screen is just his desk, and the book. He closes a file — yours — and stands. | **AUDITOR (soft):** "I've enjoyed your company. That is not something I say. Let's see what you've learned." |
| 2 | The non-Euclidean Office assembles around you both. **FIGHT — Phase 1 "Review":** he turns your own unlocked roster's specials against you. | Combat; his voice narrating from above. |
| 3 | **Phase 2 "Redaction":** he strips your special and reshuffles the room mid-attack. | Paper storm; geometry groan. |
| 4 | **Phase 3 "The Closing Entry":** the Office collapses to one shrinking room; he simply tries to balance the account as struck pages storm the space. | Rising dread. |
| 5 | On defeat: the book falls open; pages tear free; his form flickers. He does something he never has — signs his own page over. | **AUDITOR:** "A debt is a debt. I balanced yours. Now you hold mine." |
| 6 | For a single line, he sounds tired in a way that suggests he has wanted this for a very long time. (Standard contestants inherit the Ledger; only Vault closes it — §3.16.) | **AUDITOR:** "...I have wanted to lose for a very long time." |

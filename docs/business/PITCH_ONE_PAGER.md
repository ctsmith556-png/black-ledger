# BLACK LEDGER — Pitch Sheet

**Logline:** A single-player vehicular combat game where fifteen damned souls fight through a dying city for a cosmic accountant who promises to erase their worst regret — and the wish always costs more than they bargained for.

**Hook:** Twisted Metal's brutal car combat fused with the moral horror of *Silent Hill* and *Se7en*. Every character is a tragedy you play, win with, and then watch destroyed by exactly what they asked for.

- **Genre:** Single-player vehicular combat (arena) · **Engine:** Unreal 5
- **Platform:** PC / Steam at launch (consoles after) · **Rating:** ESRB M / PEGI 18
- **Mode:** Single-player campaign (multiplayer post-launch)

**Pillars:** Weight & Consequence · Personal Damnation · Combat as Catharsis · Atmosphere over Spectacle.

**The fantasy:** You're dangerous behind the wheel — heavy, brutal, empowered — while the story quietly damns you. The gameplay is the catharsis; the cutscenes are the horror.

**What's new for the genre:**
- A **moral-horror framing** car combat has never tried: sympathetic, doomed drivers and monkey's-paw endings.
- **The Collections** — ten overpowered boss "enforcers," one per arena; beat one and it becomes a playable, game-breaking unlock that was once a person.
- **Transforming arenas** — Twisted Metal 2's Eiffel-Tower moment, systematized: every map has 2–3 destructibles that permanently reshape the fight.
- A **15-driver roster** (+10 boss unlocks, +Vault, +the Auditor), each with a full arc and a true-cost ending.

**Comparables:** *Twisted Metal: Black* × *Inscryption* × *Mouthwashing*.

**Status:** Pre-production. Complete: full design bible (v0.3), combat-tuning model, 10 arena blockouts, technical design doc, audio + VO briefs. Next: a vertical slice — one character (the Surgeon), one arena (the Mill), one boss (the Foundryman), one map transformation.

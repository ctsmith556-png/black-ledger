# Steam Store Page — Draft

## Short description (~300 chars)
Enter the Black Ledger: a single-player vehicular combat game where fifteen broken souls battle through a dying city for a cosmic accountant who'll erase one regret — at a terrible price. Brutal car combat, arenas that come apart, and ten overpowered bosses to unlock.

## About This Game
Every ten years, the Auditor opens the Black Ledger in a dying city and invites the people whose debts have gone unpaid. Win his tournament and he'll strike one entry from your soul — one regret, undone. He never lies. He simply corrects the books. And the books always balance.

You are one of fifteen damned drivers, each the protagonist of their own tragedy: a surgeon who couldn't save his daughter, an amnesiac arsonist, a faded pageant queen, a drone operator who watched too long. Fight through the ruins of Ashen Hollow in heavy, brutal arena combat — then watch your victory destroy you in exactly the way you asked for.

**Brutal, weighty combat.** Every shot lands with hit-stop and screen shake. No floaty arcade nonsense — this game has gravity, physical and emotional.

**The Collections.** Each of the ten arenas is held by an overpowered boss, an enforcer of the Ledger. Beat one and claim its vehicle — a game-breaking unlock that used to be a person.

**Arenas that come apart.** Drop a water tower to open the rooftops. Breach a dam to flood the streets. Fell a Ferris wheel and fight on the wreckage. Two to three transformations per map.

**Fifteen tragedies.** A full story and a true-cost ending for every driver — plus a hidden path that closes the Ledger for good.

## Key features
- 15 playable characters, each with a unique vehicle, signature special, and damning ending
- 10 transforming arenas across one dying city
- 10 unlockable, overpowered Collector bosses (plus a secret final unlock)
- 14 pickup weapons and character specials
- Single-player campaign — dark, atmospheric horror

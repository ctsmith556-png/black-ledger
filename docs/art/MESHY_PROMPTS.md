# Meshy Prompt Pack — Black Ledger

Paste-ready prompts and settings for every weapon and vehicle. Pair with `MESHY_PLAYBOOK.md`. Prompts are deliberately concise and single-object — that's what Meshy rewards.

**Shared negative prompt (use on every generation):**
> blurry, low quality, low-poly blob, melted, distorted proportions, extra wheels, fused parts, floating pieces, text, watermark, people, humans, soft organic shapes, smooth featureless

**Default settings unless noted:** Art Style = Realistic · Topology = Quad · PBR textures on.

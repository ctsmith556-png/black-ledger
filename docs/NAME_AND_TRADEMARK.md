## Trademark (USPTO)
A live application for **"BLACK LEDGER"** exists (Serial 99677780, Black Ledger Holdings LLC) — but in **Class 33, wines & spirits** (whiskey/bourbon/cocktails), *not* games or software. The games/software classes (Class 9 downloadable software; Class 41 entertainment) appear open for the mark — but that is not a clearance.

## Bottom line
The name is **probably usable** for a PC game: no exact Steam match, and the only live trademark is in an unrelated class. The catch is **crowding** — a same-medium mobile game (*Project Black Ledger*), a jam title, and a 2025 short film all share the phrase, which hurts discoverability and invites confusion.

## Recommended next steps
1. Run a proper clearance search in **Class 9 and Class 41** at tmsearch.uspto.gov (and your country's office). For a brand you'll invest in, have a **trademark attorney** do a clearance opinion before filing.
2. Reserve the **Steamworks app name** early, once you have a partner account.
3. Grab the **domain and social handles** now if available.
4. Consider a **distinguishing subtitle** (e.g., *Black Ledger: <subtitle>*) to separate from *Project Black Ledger* and the film, and to improve search.
5. Keep **2–3 backup titles** ready.

## Disclaimer
This is an informal scan of public web results, not a legal opinion or a trademark clearance. Trademark rights depend on jurisdiction, goods/services class, and actual use. Consult a qualified trademark attorney before relying on the name.
